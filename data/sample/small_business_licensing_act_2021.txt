THE FICTIONAL SMALL BUSINESS LICENSING ACT, 2021
Jurisdiction: Republic of Veridia
Enacted: 21 January 2021

CHAPTER I — PRELIMINARY

Section 1. Short title and application.
(1) This Act may be called the Small Business Licensing Act, 2021.
(2) It applies to every business enterprise employing fewer than fifty persons within the Republic of Veridia.

Section 2. Definitions.
(1) "Licensing authority" means the municipal office designated to grant, renew, or revoke business licenses under this Act.
(2) "Small enterprise" means a business enterprise employing fewer than fifty persons and having annual turnover below fifty lakh Veridian Marks.

CHAPTER II — LICENSING PROCEDURE

Section 3. Requirement to obtain a license.
This section requires every small enterprise to obtain a license from the licensing authority before commencing operations.
Clause (a): An application for a license must be submitted at least thirty days before the intended date of commencement.
Clause (b): The licensing authority must issue a decision within twenty-one days of receiving a complete application.

Section 4. Fees for licensing.
This section fixes the fee schedule payable by a small enterprise when applying for or renewing a license.
Clause (a): The initial license fee is capped at five thousand Veridian Marks for enterprises employing fewer than ten persons.
Clause (b): The renewal fee, payable annually, shall not exceed half the initial license fee.

Section 5. Grounds for refusal.
This section lists the grounds on which the licensing authority may refuse a license application.
Clause (a): The premises fail to meet the fire safety standards prescribed by the Municipal Safety Code.
Clause (b): The applicant has had a prior license revoked under Section 6 within the preceding three years.

CHAPTER III — REVOCATION AND APPEALS

Section 6. Revocation of license.
This section empowers the licensing authority to revoke a license where the small enterprise is found operating in violation of the conditions attached to the license.
Clause (a): The licensing authority must give the enterprise fourteen days written notice and an opportunity to be heard before revocation.
Clause (b): A revoked license may not be reissued to the same applicant for a period of one year.

Section 7. Appeal against refusal or revocation.
This section allows a small enterprise aggrieved by a refusal under Section 5 or a revocation under Section 6 to appeal to the Municipal Licensing Tribunal within forty-five days.
