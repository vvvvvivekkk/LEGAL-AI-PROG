THE FICTIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL WASTE MANAGEMENT ACT, 2022
Jurisdiction: State of Larenthia
Enacted: 15 September 2022

CHAPTER I — PRELIMINARY

Section 1. Short title and commencement.
(1) This Act may be called the Environmental Waste Management Act, 2022.
(2) It shall come into force on 1 January 2023.

Section 2. Definitions.
(1) "Waste generator" means any person or establishment that produces solid or liquid waste as a by-product of its activity.
(2) "Hazardous waste" means waste that, by reason of its chemical, physical, or biological composition, poses a substantial threat to public health or the environment.

CHAPTER II — SEGREGATION AND DISPOSAL

Section 3. Duty to segregate waste at source.
This section requires every waste generator to segregate waste into biodegradable, non-biodegradable, and hazardous categories at the point of generation.
Clause (a): Mixed disposal of segregated categories is prohibited and constitutes an offence under Section 7.
Clause (b): Local bodies must provide separate collection facilities for each category within their jurisdiction.

Section 4. Disposal of hazardous waste.
This section governs how a waste generator must handle and dispose of hazardous waste identified under Section 2(2).
Clause (a): Hazardous waste must be handed over only to a disposal agency authorized under Section 5.
Clause (b): Storage of hazardous waste on the generator's premises shall not exceed ninety days without written permission from the State Pollution Control Board.

CHAPTER III — AUTHORIZATION OF DISPOSAL AGENCIES

Section 5. Authorization of disposal agencies.
This section requires any agency engaged in the collection, transport, or disposal of hazardous waste to obtain authorization from the State Pollution Control Board.
Clause (a): Authorization is valid for a period of three years and must be renewed at least sixty days before expiry.
Clause (b): The Board may inspect an authorized agency's facilities at any time without prior notice.

CHAPTER IV — OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

Section 6. Penalty for improper segregation.
This section fixes the fine payable by a waste generator found in violation of the segregation duty under Section 3.
Clause (a): A first violation attracts a fine of up to ten thousand Veridian Marks.
Clause (b): A repeat violation within one year attracts a fine of up to fifty thousand Veridian Marks.

Section 7. Offence of mixed disposal.
This section makes it an offence for any waste generator to dispose of mixed, unsegregated waste in violation of Section 3(a), punishable with the fine prescribed under Section 6.

Section 8. Appeals.
This section allows any person aggrieved by an order of the State Pollution Control Board under this Act to appeal to the Environmental Appellate Tribunal within thirty days.
