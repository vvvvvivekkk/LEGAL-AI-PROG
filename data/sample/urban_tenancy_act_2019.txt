THE FICTIONAL URBAN TENANCY ACT, 2019
Jurisdiction: State of Larenthia
Enacted: 4 July 2019

CHAPTER I — PRELIMINARY

Section 1. Short title, extent and commencement.
(1) This Act may be called the Urban Tenancy Act, 2019.
(2) It extends to all urban local body areas within the State of Larenthia.

Section 2. Definitions.
(1) "Landlord" means a person who leases residential premises to a tenant in exchange for rent.
(2) "Tenant" means a person who occupies residential premises under a lease from a landlord.
(3) "Rent authority" means the officer appointed under Section 11 to adjudicate tenancy disputes.

CHAPTER II — RENT AGREEMENTS

Section 3. Written rent agreement mandatory.
This section requires every tenancy exceeding thirty days to be recorded in a written rent agreement registered with the rent authority.
Clause (a): The agreement must state the monthly rent, the security deposit, and the duration of the tenancy.
Clause (b): An unregistered rent agreement exceeding thirty days is not enforceable against the tenant for the purpose of eviction proceedings.

Section 4. Security deposit limits.
This section caps the security deposit a landlord may collect from a tenant of residential premises.
Clause (a): The security deposit shall not exceed two months' rent for residential premises.
Clause (b): The landlord must refund the security deposit within thirty days of the tenant vacating the premises, less any lawful deductions.

CHAPTER III — EVICTION AND DISPUTES

Section 5. Grounds for eviction.
This section lists the exclusive grounds on which a landlord may seek eviction of a tenant through the rent authority.
Clause (a): Non-payment of rent for two consecutive months after written notice.
Clause (b): Use of the premises for a purpose materially different from that agreed in the rent agreement.
Clause (c): Bona fide requirement of the premises by the landlord for personal occupation.

Section 6. Notice period before eviction.
This section requires a landlord to give the tenant no less than ninety days written notice before filing an eviction application with the rent authority, except in cases of non-payment of rent.

Section 7. Penalty for illegal eviction.
This section fixes the penalty payable by a landlord who forcibly evicts a tenant without an order from the rent authority.
Clause (a): A landlord found to have carried out an illegal eviction shall pay the tenant compensation equal to six months' rent.
Clause (b): Repeated violations may result in suspension of the landlord's registration under Section 3.

CHAPTER IV — RENT AUTHORITY

Section 8. Appointment of rent authority.
This section provides for the State Government to appoint one or more rent authorities for each urban local body area.

Section 9. Powers of the rent authority.
This section empowers the rent authority to summon parties, examine documents, and pass binding orders on tenancy disputes referred to it under this Act.

Section 10. Appeals.
This section allows any party aggrieved by an order of the rent authority to appeal to the District Court within sixty days of the order.

Section 11. Appointment procedure.
This section describes the qualifications and procedure for appointing officers as rent authorities under Section 8.
