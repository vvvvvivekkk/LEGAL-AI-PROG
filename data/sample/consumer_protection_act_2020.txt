THE FICTIONAL CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2020
Jurisdiction: Republic of Veridia
Enacted: 9 November 2020

CHAPTER I — PRELIMINARY

Section 1. Short title and commencement.
(1) This Act may be called the Consumer Protection Act, 2020.
(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette of Veridia.

Section 2. Definitions.
(1) "Consumer" means a person who buys goods or hires services for consideration, other than for resale or commercial purpose.
(2) "Unfair trade practice" means a deceptive practice adopted by a seller for the purpose of promoting the sale of goods or services.

CHAPTER II — CONSUMER RIGHTS

Section 3. Right to information.
This section grants every consumer the right to be informed of the quality, quantity, purity, standard, and price of goods or services before purchase.
Clause (a): Sellers must display the maximum retail price on all packaged goods offered for sale.
Clause (b): Withholding material information about a defect known to the seller constitutes an unfair trade practice under Section 2(2).

Section 4. Right to redress against defective goods.
This section entitles a consumer to seek replacement, repair, or refund for goods found to be defective within the warranty period.
Clause (a): A seller must respond to a redress request within fifteen days of receipt.
Clause (b): Failure to respond within the prescribed period entitles the consumer to approach the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission established under Section 6.

CHAPTER III — UNFAIR TRADE PRACTICES

Section 5. Prohibition of unfair trade practices.
This section prohibits any seller from engaging in an unfair trade practice as defined under Section 2(2), including false advertising and misleading claims of quality.
Clause (a): A seller found to have engaged in an unfair trade practice is liable to a penalty of up to one lakh Veridian Marks for a first offence.
Clause (b): A repeat offence within two years is liable to a penalty of up to five lakh Veridian Marks and suspension of trading license.

CHAPTER IV — CONSUMER DISPUTES REDRESSAL COMMISSION

Section 6. Establishment of the Commission.
This section establishes the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission to adjudicate complaints filed by consumers under this Act.

Section 7. Jurisdiction of the Commission.
This section defines the pecuniary and territorial jurisdiction of the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission for complaints filed under Sections 4 and 5.

Section 8. Appeals.
This section allows any party aggrieved by an order of the Commission to appeal to the State Consumer Appellate Tribunal within thirty days of the order.
