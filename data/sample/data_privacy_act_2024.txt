THE FICTIONAL DATA PRIVACY ACT, 2024
Jurisdiction: Republic of Veridia
Enacted: 12 March 2024

CHAPTER I — PRELIMINARY

Section 1. Short title and commencement.
(1) This Act may be called the Data Privacy Act, 2024.
(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette of Veridia.

Section 2. Definitions.
(1) In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, "personal data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
(2) "Data fiduciary" means any person who, alone or in conjunction with others, determines the purpose and means of processing personal data.
(3) "Data principal" means the natural person to whom the personal data relates.

CHAPTER II — OBLIGATIONS OF DATA FIDUCIARIES

Section 3. Grounds for processing personal data.
This section sets out the lawful grounds on which a data fiduciary may process personal data belonging to a data principal.
Clause (a): Processing is permitted where the data principal has given clear and specific consent.
Clause (b): Processing is permitted where necessary for compliance with a legal obligation imposed on the data fiduciary.
Clause (c): Processing is permitted where necessary to respond to a medical emergency involving a threat to the life of the data principal.

Section 4. Notice requirements.
This section requires every data fiduciary to give the data principal a clear notice before or at the time of collecting personal data.
Clause (a): The notice must describe the personal data being collected and the purpose of processing.
Clause (b): The notice must describe the manner in which the data principal may exercise their rights under Section 7.
Clause (c): The notice must identify the grievance officer designated under Section 9.

CHAPTER III — DATA BREACH AND PENALTIES

Section 5. Duty to report personal data breach.
This section requires a data fiduciary to notify the Data Protection Board of Veridia and affected data principals without undue delay upon becoming aware of a personal data breach.
Clause (a): Notification to the Board must occur within seventy-two hours of the data fiduciary becoming aware of the breach.
Clause (b): Notification must describe the nature of the breach and the categories of personal data affected.

Section 6. Penalty for failure to report a breach.
This section fixes the monetary penalty a data fiduciary must pay for failing to notify a personal data breach as required under Section 5.
Clause (a): Failure to notify within the prescribed period attracts a penalty of up to two hundred fifty crore Veridian Marks.
Clause (b): The Board may reduce the penalty where the data fiduciary demonstrates reasonable efforts at compliance.

CHAPTER IV — RIGHTS OF DATA PRINCIPALS

Section 7. Right to access and correction.
This section grants every data principal the right to obtain confirmation of whether their personal data is being processed, and to request correction of inaccurate personal data.
Clause (a): A data fiduciary must respond to an access request within thirty days.
Clause (b): A data fiduciary must correct inaccurate personal data within fifteen days of a verified request.

Section 8. Right to erasure.
This section grants a data principal the right to request erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected.
Clause (a): The data fiduciary must comply with an erasure request unless retention is required by another law.

Section 9. Grievance officer.
This section requires every data fiduciary to designate a grievance officer to address complaints from data principals within thirty days of receipt.
